Privacy Policy for Smart Kids

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Tharindu Wijerathna ("we," "our," or "us") built the Smart Kids mobile application as a free educational app designed for children and families. This page informs visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information (such as name, address, phone number, or email) from children or users. All game progress and character selections are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google Play Families Policy)

Our app uses Google AdMob to display kid-safe rewarded advertisements. We configure Google AdMob strictly in compliance with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA:

  • Child-directed treatment is enabled (tagForChildDirectedTreatment).
  • Only General Audiences (G-rated) and non-personalized ads are requested.
  • Advertising identifiers are not used to build user profiles or track children across apps.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: https://google.com

3. Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children's privacy online. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided any personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
